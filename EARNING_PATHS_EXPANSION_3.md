# Earning Paths Expansion 3 — More Markets, Grants, Competitions and Product Routes

## Physical-world / local-business service ideas an AI + human team could sell

These are service concepts rather than platform endorsements. The agent can prospect, quote, prepare documents and automate reporting; the human performs site visits, physical work and regulated tasks only when qualified.

- Small-business website maintenance and accessibility remediation.
- Local business Google Business Profile / directory data cleanup with owner authorization.
- Menu, inventory and product-catalog digitization.
- Appointment reminder and missed-call workflow setup.
- Invoice/receipt organization and spreadsheet automation.
- Basic inventory reorder dashboards.
- Equipment maintenance schedule and parts catalog.
- Solar/battery usage dashboard using customer-provided meter data.
- Home-network documentation and device inventory.
- Small office backup verification and restore drills.
- 3D-printable replacement-part modeling from owner-supplied measurements, with safety-critical parts excluded.
- CAD drawing cleanup and BOM preparation for review by a qualified engineer.
- Product photography batch organization and catalog entry.
- Authorized property listing data cleanup and floor-plan redrawing from supplied plans.
- Local contractor quote comparison and materials takeoff assistance (not engineering certification).
- Public-record research and permit-status tracking where lawful.
- Website change monitoring and monthly report.
- Restaurant menu price monitoring using permitted public sources.
- Small-business FAQ chatbot built on owner-approved documents, with human escalation.
- Customer support inbox triage and draft replies with owner approval.
- Automated review-response drafts; never fabricate reviews or post without authorization.
- Accessibility checks and remediation plans.
- Spreadsheet-to-dashboard conversion.
- QR-code menus and catalog systems.
- Local nonprofit grant calendar and application-draft assistance.
- Workshop asset register, tool checkout and maintenance logs.

## Recurring income models worth testing

1. Fixed-scope setup fee + monthly maintenance.
2. Monthly monitoring/report subscription.
3. Usage-metered API or data feed.
4. Per-document/per-record processing with a minimum monthly commitment.
5. Support retainer with a defined hours cap.
6. Paid plugin/extension with free basic tier.
7. Open-source project + hosted managed version.
8. Paid templates, CAD assets, scripts or workflow packs.
9. Commission on verified referrals only where legally permitted and disclosed.
10. Sponsored maintenance of a useful open-source tool.

## New daemon discovery instructions

For every platform, record the official URL, last checked date, original terms URL, whether autonomous agents are expressly allowed, whether a human account holder is required, reward type, funding evidence, payout rails, fees, minimum withdrawal, geography, task freshness and a test task. Keep separate labels for cash, stablecoins, volatile tokens, cloud credits, points and reputation. Do not count credits or unwithdrawable tokens as revenue.

For every service idea, find at least three examples of real buyers or public requests before building a product. Prefer customer interviews or a small paid pilot over building an unrequested tool. Keep a record of time spent prospecting, conversion rate, accepted deliverables and settled net revenue.
